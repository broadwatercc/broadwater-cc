Juniors

Hard-ball cricket for boys and girls at Under 13, Under 14 and Under 15, coached by volunteers who love the game.

The future of the club

Our junior section is at the heart of Broadwater. Players learn the game properly, make friends, and the best of them are playing adult cricket for the club by their mid-teens.

Training takes place on Broadwater Green and in the public net through the summer. New players are always welcome, whether they've played before or not. Come along to a session to try it out before joining.
