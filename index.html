A community club on the town's green

We've played on the same patch of grass since 1771, which makes Broadwater the first cricket club formed in what is now the borough of Worthing.

Today we run three Saturday sides in the Sussex Cricket League, a Sunday friendly XI, a midweek T20 team and an ever-growing junior section with U13, U14 and U15 teams. Whether you're an experienced league cricketer, returning to the game or picking up a bat for the first time, there's a game here for you.

Broadwater Green is a public ground, and the net on the green is the only public cricket net in Worthing: it's there for everyone.

255 years on Broadwater Green

Cricket was being played on the green in the 1720s. Half a century later, the club was formed, and it has been here ever since.

  1. 1720sFirst recorded cricket on Broadwater Green, the earliest sport recorded in Worthing.
  2. 1771Broadwater Cricket Club is founded and plays its first match.
  3. 1890sThe Broadwater and Worthing club divides, and Broadwater stays on the green.
  4. TodayThree Saturday league sides and a flourishing junior section rebuilt by our volunteers.
