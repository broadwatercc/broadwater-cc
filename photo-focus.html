Photo focus

Choose which part of each photo stays in view when the website crops it. This page is just for you; it isn't linked from the site.

  1. Click on the most important part of each photo, such as the players' faces. The previews show how it will look in a wide banner and a tall panel.
  2. When you've done them all, click Copy settings at the bottom of the screen.
  3. On GitHub, open the js folder, click photos.js, then the pencil icon. Press Ctrl + A, then Ctrl + V to paste, and click Commit changes.

Your clicks are remembered on this computer, so you can come back and adjust them later.
